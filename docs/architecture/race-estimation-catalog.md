# Race estimation calculation catalogue

Version `race_estimation_v2`. **75 model definitions**. Generated by `backend/scripts/export_race_estimation_catalog.py`.

The existing 137 general metrics and 67 strategy definitions remain available. This catalogue adds composable physical scenarios and panel snapshots; overlapping concepts are not additional independent measurements.

[Architecture, exact algorithms, assumptions, limitations and extension runbook](12-race-estimation.md).

`observed` = source observation, `derived` = numerical calculation, `scenario` = explicit physical/strategy assumptions, `index` = uncalibrated relative score. Unavailable inputs produce null values, not synthetic observations. Support is evidence coverage and heuristic evidence strength, never a calibrated event probability.

## Wind Physics

**Required inputs:** fresh weather; fresh car speed; north transform and wind convention for vector components.

**Consumer / purpose:** Aerodynamic scenario inputs and wind confounder context; never an automatic lap-time correction.

| ID | Label | Unit | Method | Calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `crosswind_ms` | Crosswind component | m/s | scenario | Wind magnitude * sin(wind FROM bearing - calibrated car bearing); unavailable without north and direction-convention calibration. |
| `relative_air_speed_kph` | Relative airspeed scenario | kph | scenario | 3.6 * sqrt((ground_speed + headwind)^2 + crosswind^2); explicit alignment calibration required. |
| `dynamic_pressure_pa` | Aerodynamic dynamic pressure | Pa | scenario | 0.5 * density * airspeed^2. Ground speed is the central proxy without wind alignment; magnitude bounds use v +/- wind. |
| `wind_pressure_shift_pct` | Wind dynamic-pressure change | % | scenario | 100 * ((relative_air_speed / ground_speed)^2 - 1); calibrated wind components required, ground speed at least 40 kph; not a lap-time penalty. |

## Aero

**Consumer / purpose:** Compare alternative wake/air-load assumptions and explain potential cornering or tow effects.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `wake_loss_scenario_pct` | Wake downforce-loss scenario | % | scenario | min(max_loss, loss_at_10m * exp(-(distance-10)/decay)); design prior, unknown lateral alignment. |
| `corner_speed_loss_scenario_pct` | Corner-speed sensitivity | % | scenario | 100*(1-sqrt((1+downforce_ratio*(1-wake_loss))/(1+downforce_ratio))); quasi-static grip limit. |

## Interpretation limits

- All calculations stop at the replay cutoff; source reports have a configurable publication margin.
- A 0.3-0.4 second gap indicates close traffic, not proof that dirty air caused a pace change.
- The wake prior is inspired by published 2022 design research, not calibrated for a specific 2023-2025 car.
- Brake is binary. Wheel lock, tyre temperature, actual wear and collision impact cannot be observed directly.
- Indices are relative heuristic scores; support is data coverage rather than event probability.
- XYZ does not identify lateral placement or track edges. Wind incidence needs a calibrated north transform.
