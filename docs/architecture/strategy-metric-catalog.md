# Strategy calculation catalogue

Version `strategy_foundation_v1`. **67 decision-oriented calculations**, generated by `backend/scripts/export_strategy_catalog.py`.

These are the primary strategy primitives. [Architecture, exact methods, worked examples and extension runbook](10-strategy-foundation.md). Existing generic diagnostic statistics remain in [the compatibility catalogue](f1-metric-catalog.md).

Every value has an event-time cutoff, entry/pair/session scope, units, evidence and availability. `estimated` and `assumed` are never displayed as direct measurements. A percentage is a documented fraction or an evidence-backed conditional probability, never a renamed confidence score.

## Pace

**Required inputs:** completed clean laps <= as_of.

**Interpretation limits:** Lap time is observed; causal tyre/fuel/weather attribution needs identifiable calibration.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `strategy_clean_laps` | entry | laps | Count accurate non-pit, non-deleted/generated laps with explicit clear track flag. | Sample support for every pace decision. |
| `strategy_recent_pace_s` | entry | s/lap | Median of last five eligible clean laps. | Baseline race pace and pit projections. |
| `strategy_pace_scatter_s` | entry | s | 1.4826 * median absolute deviation of last ten clean lap times. | Robust pace sensitivity and uncertainty. |
| `strategy_pace_trend_s_per_lap` | entry | s/lap | Theil-Sen median pairwise slope over last ten clean lap times versus lap number. | Detect pace trend without pretending it is pure tyre degradation. |
| `strategy_last_lap_residual_s` | entry | s | Latest clean lap minus median of the preceding five clean laps. | Flag unusual performance; check traffic/weather before attributing a mistake. |
| `strategy_current_compound` | entry | compound | Compound from the latest already completed lap. | Known tyre state; does not infer an unseen tyre change. |
| `strategy_reported_tyre_age_laps` | entry | laps | Reported tyre life on latest completed lap. | Initialize tyre curves without using future lap metadata. |
| `strategy_common_lap_trend_s_per_lap` | entry | s/lap | OLS common race-lap coefficient with driver, compound and compound-specific tyre-age effects. | Separate common fuel/evolution trend from wear when the design is identifiable. |
| `strategy_compound_age_slopes` | entry | s/lap | OLS compound-specific age coefficients and 95% coefficient intervals from full-rank standardized design. | Calibrate tyre scenarios; coefficient remains conditional and observational. |
| `strategy_pace_design_condition` | entry | ratio | Largest divided by smallest singular value of standardized pace design. | Reject collinear tyre/fuel models rather than manufacture precise coefficients. |

## Rivals

**Required inputs:** matched lap/sector crossings, fresh bounded positions.

**Interpretation limits:** Positive time gap means focus is behind rival. Sector gaps are measured at equal timing lines, not simultaneously. Arc distance is geometry-estimated.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rival_timing_gap_s` | pair | s | Latest t_focus(crossing)-t_rival(same crossing), both observed by cutoff. | Anchor rival gap to an actual shared timing line. |
| `rival_gap_observation_age_s` | pair | s | (as_of - max crossing timestamp)/1000. | Expose staleness before pit-window decisions. |
| `rival_mean_timing_gap_s` | pair | s | Mean of up to twenty latest matched timing-line gaps. | Compare sustained separation rather than one sample. |
| `rival_closing_rate_s_per_s` | pair | s/s | Negative Theil-Sen slope of matched clean end-lap gap versus observation clock. | Project a gap trend; positive means focus is closing. |
| `rival_pace_advantage_s_per_lap` | pair | s/lap | Median(rival clean lap - focus clean lap) on matching lap numbers, latest five. | Estimate undercut pressure from comparable laps. |
| `rival_projected_gap_s` | pair | s | Last gap - closing_rate*age, at most one pace lap old; reject pit/neutralized changes after anchor. | Conditional gap at decision time with sensitivity bounds. |
| `rival_catch_time_s` | pair | s | Projected positive gap / positive closing rate. | Time to catch at unchanged pace; not an overtake guarantee. |
| `rival_crossings_inside_2s_pct` | pair | % | 100*count(0<matched_gap<=2)/number of matched crossings. | Observed close-following exposure; not an inferred probability. |
| `rival_mean_euclidean_distance_m` | pair | m | Mean simultaneous fresh interpolated XY separation on a shared one-second clock. | Spatial context with adjacent-track/pit-lane caveats. |
| `rival_min_euclidean_distance_m` | pair | m | Minimum simultaneous bounded XY separation. | Locate proximity episodes without claiming contact. |
| `rival_arc_ahead_m` | pair | m | (rival lap progress - focus lap progress)*reference length at same fresh timestamp. | Along-track separation with lap count, avoiding Euclidean shortcuts. |
| `rival_mean_arc_ahead_m` | pair | m | Mean plausible simultaneous signed race-distance difference. | Time-series distance groundwork for traffic and blue-flag scenarios. |
| `rival_pair_coverage_pct` | pair | % | 100*valid simultaneous sampled instants/requested instants. | Exclude stale tracking from race-state decisions. |

## Pit

**Required inputs:** completed pit visits, comparable clean in/out-lap neighbors, known external stationary reports.

**Interpretation limits:** Pit-lane time, stationary service time and net race-time loss are different quantities. Retrospective estimates only use already completed laps.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pit_latest_lane_duration_s` | entry | s | Latest observed paired pit-out minus pit-in. | Baseline total visit and detect abnormal delay. |
| `pit_field_lane_baseline_s` | entry | s | Median completed comparable same-phase lane visits, at least three. | Reference for a rival's unusually long visit. |
| `pit_latest_lane_excess_s` | entry | s | Latest lane duration minus comparable field median. | Visit delay; cannot alone identify a slow wheel change. |
| `pit_latest_stationary_duration_s` | entry | s | Latest source-reported stationary duration known by cutoff. | Separate service from pit-lane travel when provider supports it. |
| `pit_stationary_excess_s` | entry | s | Reported stationary duration minus median other comparable known service reports (n>=3). | Identify service-time loss rather than confusing it with lane travel. |
| `pit_net_loss_estimate_s` | entry | s | Median[(inlap+outlap) - (preceding clean lap + subsequent clean lap)], n>=3 green visits. | Empirical net-loss candidate; neighbour tyre, traffic and warmup effects remain confounders. |
| `pit_net_loss_scatter_s` | entry | s | 1.4826*MAD of eligible paired-lap net-loss observations. | Pit scenario sensitivity; not calibrated coverage. |
| `pit_green_loss_input_s` | entry | s | Validated context green net loss, else supported empirical estimate. | Cost to add to race-time strategy projections. |
| `pit_sc_discount_s` | entry | s | Configured green net loss minus configured SC net loss. | Compare actual/conditional neutralized stop cost. |
| `pit_vsc_discount_s` | entry | s | Configured green net loss minus configured VSC net loss. | VSC pit-cost scenario without a fixed universal discount. |
| `pit_next_entry_distance_m` | entry | m | Forward reference-line distance from fresh car position to verified pit-entry progress. | Know whether the pit entry is still ahead this lap. |
| `pit_next_entry_eta_s` | entry | s | Historical reference-lap forward transit time scaled by current median pace; average-lap fallback is labelled. | Propagate closing gaps to the next reachable pit decision boundary. |

## Pit Windows

**Required inputs:** fresh rival gap, net pit-loss model, completed rival stop.

**Interpretation limits:** Counterfactuals are conditional. Never subtract a rival's stop twice after the measured gap already contains it.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pit_rejoin_gap_vs_rival_s` | pair | s | Projected focus gap + focus net pit loss. | Positive means focus would rejoin behind this rival if rival stays out. |
| `pit_free_stop_margin_s` | pair | s | -(projected focus gap + focus pit loss). | Positive reserve before losing position to this rival. |
| `pit_rival_normal_stop_counterfactual_gap_s` | pair | s | Actual current gap + rival's independently observed excess visit/service delay. | Reconstruct a normal-stop counterfactual after actual gap has incorporated the stop. |
| `pit_rival_delay_creates_window` | pair | boolean | Actual rejoin upper bound<0 AND normal-stop counterfactual rejoin lower bound>=0. | Detect the specific opportunity created only by a rival's abnormal completed stop. |
| `pit_undercut_break_even_laps` | pair | laps | First horizon where sum(old-rival projected lap - fresh-focus projected lap) exceeds positive current gap. | Conditional tyre-only undercut threshold; overtaking and rival response are separate. |

## Spatial Incident

**Required inputs:** fresh telemetry, verified same-frame track/recovery geometry.

**Interpretation limits:** Position is approximate. Spatial classes use an explicit error band; neither a racing-line deviation nor speed drop confirms a crash.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `incident_max_short_drop_kph` | entry | km/h | Maximum speed decline over <=2s of continuous plausible samples in latest 30s. | Trigger incident inspection while retaining ordinary-braking explanations. |
| `incident_current_stop_duration_s` | entry | s | Continuous latest observed speed<=5 km/h, no held gaps and fresh end observation. | Distinguish momentary braking from a stopped obstruction. |
| `incident_motion_state` | entry | state | Freshness, pit, slow movement, sustained stop and verified surface/recovery context. | Explain candidate collision/retirement/recovery state without declaring mechanical cause. |
| `incident_surface_clearance_m` | entry | m | Signed distance to union of supplied racing-surface polygons; positive inside. | Distinguish confidently inside/outside from boundary-uncertain tracking. |
| `incident_nearest_recovery_m` | entry | m | Minimum straight-line distance to supplied recovery access points. | Input to calibrated incident cohorts; not actual accessible recovery path length. |
| `incident_candidate_collision` | entry | boolean | Rapid drop>=100 km/h followed by >=4s fresh stop, outside known pits, with spatial context. | Corroborated incident candidate for risk evaluation; never a confirmed crash. |
| `incident_retirement_candidate` | entry | boolean | Fresh sustained stop>=30s outside pit with stopped-off-track/recovery spatial evidence. | Retirement suspicion; loss of telemetry alone is not retirement. |

## Weather Physics

**Required inputs:** fresh speed/heading, fresh wind/pressure/temperature, verified north alignment.

**Interpretation limits:** Weather station wind is not local car airflow. No car aero polar means no causal lap-time correction or tyre-blame attribution.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `weather_headwind_mps` | entry | m/s | Minus wind velocity dotted with car heading; weather direction is where wind comes FROM. | Longitudinal aerodynamic exposure on this track segment. |
| `weather_crosswind_right_mps` | entry | m/s | Wind velocity dotted with car's right unit vector. | Direction-sensitive yaw/cornering exposure. |
| `weather_relative_air_speed_mps` | entry | m/s | Magnitude(v_ground_vector - wind_vector). | Correct velocity input to aerodynamic pressure. |
| `weather_relative_air_yaw_deg` | entry | deg | atan2(relative-air-motion right component, forward component). | Aero-yaw scenario input, not measured steering/yaw. |
| `weather_moist_air_density_kg_m3` | entry | kg/m3 | (pressure-vapour)/(287.05*T_K) + vapour/(461.495*T_K), vapour from humidity and saturation pressure. | Density groundwork for pressure and force models. |
| `weather_dynamic_pressure_pa` | entry | Pa | 0.5*rho*relative_air_speed^2. | Physical aero input; force additionally requires area and coefficients. |
| `weather_dynamic_pressure_change_pct` | entry | % | 100*(relative_air_speed^2/ground_speed^2 - 1). | Relative pressure exposure, explicitly not a lap-time percentage. |
| `vehicle_heading_xy_deg` | entry | deg | atan2(dy,dx) from fresh plausible movement. | Track-local direction, independent of north calibration. |
| `vehicle_turn_direction` | entry | direction | Sign of continuous wrapped change in path heading; positive=left in right-handed XY. | Cornering context; not steering wheel angle. |

## Safety Car

**Required inputs:** incident state, prior same-circuit labelled incident cohort, fixed label horizon.

**Interpretation limits:** Empirical conditional probabilities, not exact predictions. Cohort labels must predate this race; no uncalibrated weighted score is called a probability.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `incident_sc_probability_pct` | session | % | 100*(n_any_SC+1)/(n+2), Beta(1,1) posterior mean; every cohort row must have known SC occurrence. | Conditional P(SC within horizon / stopped/surface/recovery cohort). |
| `incident_vsc_probability_pct` | session | % | 100*(n_any_VSC+1)/(n+2), Beta(1,1); VSC and SC may both occur within the horizon. | Keep VSC distinct from full SC. |
| `incident_red_probability_pct` | session | % | 100*(n_red+1)/(n+2), Beta(1,1) conditional estimate. | Expose suspension risk separately; no generic pit saving assumed. |
| `incident_no_neutralisation_probability_pct` | session | % | 100*(n_none+1)/(n+2), Beta(1,1) conditional estimate. | Chance of no intervention; separate occurrence probabilities need not sum to 100%. |
| `incident_response_distribution_pct` | session | % | 100*(n_class+1)/(n+4), Dirichlet(1,1,1,1) over mutually exclusive highest response: red > SC > VSC > none. | A separate four-class outcome distribution that sums to 100%; SC then red belongs to red here, but counts as SC occurrence above. |
| `incident_risk_cohort_n` | session | incidents | Count unique earlier same-circuit incidents with identical state bins and label horizon. | Expose evidence before displaying a percentage. |
| `incident_risk_brier` | session | score | Chronological expanding-window mean squared error of four-outcome Dirichlet predictions on held-out rows. | Prequential calibration diagnostic; lower is better, not a guarantee. |

## Planning

**Required inputs:** tyre curves/inventory, pit-loss model, remaining laps, explicit rule profile.

**Interpretation limits:** Best means minimum modeled elapsed time among bounded legal configured plans. Race-wide traffic, passes, unknown tyres and future flags are not solved by this primitive.

| ID | Scope | Unit | Calculation | Strategy/user use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `strategy_best_gain_vs_stay_out_s` | entry | s | Stay-out modeled time minus lowest feasible candidate modeled time. | Quantify tyre/pit tradeoff under declared scenario assumptions. |
| `strategy_first_stop_window_laps` | entry | laps | Minimum/maximum first-stop offsets among plans within max(1s,best sensitivity width) of best. | Near-optimal pit window, not a falsely exact mandatory lap. |
| `strategy_feasible_plan_count` | entry | plans | Number of evaluated plans satisfying configured inventory, tyre-age and race-rule constraints. | Show search support and expose unavailable inputs. |
| `strategy_rejoin_rank_estimate` | entry | position | 1+number of selected field rivals with projected rejoin gap>0, only if all roster gaps fresh. | Conditional rejoin order with complete field coverage. |
