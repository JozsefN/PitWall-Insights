# Replay strategy validation

The offline fixtures store lap and sector durations, pit reports and race-control timestamps from four OpenF1 sessions. Professional reports identify the events and inform the expected relationships. Timing fixtures supply the clocks and numerical measurements. [OpenF1 laps and sector timing](https://openf1.org/docs/#laps) · [OpenF1 pit reports](https://openf1.org/docs/#pit)

| Case | Regression checks |
| --- | --- |
| Imola 2025 | Norris stopped before the VSC and Verstappen stopped after it. Pirelli's Friday estimate was around 27 seconds under green. The sector model measures 28.26 s under green and 22.18 s in stable VSC windows across the field. The replay at the warning cutoff cannot access those later VSC stops. [Pirelli debrief](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-friday-in-emilia-romagna-2025.5ldn5LvjvLOjI2E16kLZgU) · [FIA stop report](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2025_07_ita_f1_r0_timing_racepitstopsummary_v01_0.pdf) · [Peter Windsor’s race debrief](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rYijBMKrKsw) |
| Bahrain 2025 | Verstappen's two stationary stops were 4.7 s and 6.2 s in the feed. Piastri's first stop preceded the Safety Car and his second took place during it. The timing model measures 23.73 s under green against Formula 1's published 22.9 s estimate. [Formula 1 race report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/piastri-storms-to-controlled-victory-in-bahrain-grand-prix-ahead-of-russell.47YQh0Ex2gkZcx58fRaRqJ) · [Formula 1 pre-race analysis](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/need-to-know-the-most-important-facts-stats-and-trivia-ahead-of-the-2025-bahrain-grand-prix.2UBRH78pLhBrYbZoChAvHZ) |
| Miami 2025 | McLaren's two stops on lap 29 were reported 11.46 s apart during the same VSC. The test checks order, lap and timing. [McLaren race report](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/formula-1/2025/miami-grand-prix/race-report/) |
| Melbourne 2026 | The feed uses the short `VSC DEPLOYED` and `VSC ENDING` messages. During the second VSC, pit entry closed at 04:31:16 UTC and reopened at 04:32:21 UTC. The planner must remove the immediate stop throughout the closure. [Mark Hughes’ strategy debrief](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/monday-morning-debrief-could-ferrari-have-won-in-australia-with-a-different.6yQwaJxaJCZdS16fS2jcIw) · [Formula 1 Mercedes double-stack clip](https://www.formula1.com/en/video/2026-australian-grand-prix-mercedes-pull-off-double-stack-pit-stop.1859070060304484943) · [Ruth Buscombe’s strategy debrief](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AzILFziaTmA) |

The sector archive yielded observations from 82 races. With the target race's measured green cost supplied to both methods, the historical adjustment lowered mean absolute error from **5.41 s to 2.77 s** over 22 Safety Car tests and from **7.25 s to 2.72 s** over eight VSC tests. Melbourne's first-weekend example still shows the uncertainty of transferring prior data. The adjustment estimated 21.46 s against a measured 15.38 s VSC cohort. Melbourne's actual mixed-phase traffic and pit-lane closure are modelled separately from that aggregate. [Pit-cost backtest](pit-sector-backtest.json)

Run the offline regressions from `backend` with `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_professional_strategy_cases.py -v`. Rebuild fixtures with `python -m scripts.build_strategy_case_fixtures --cache-dir <path>`. From the repository root, `python scripts/check_replay_strategy.py` runs the backend and browser workflow against stored OpenF1 sessions. ## Weather and out-laps

- Bahrain 2025 and Silverstone 2024 supplied usable S2 out-lap timing **22.84 s** and **24.16 s** before the completed lap. The out-lap check uses the normalized crossing timestamp and one-second buffer. The visible dashboard can add up to five seconds of replay sampling delay. [Timing measurements](weather-crossover-timing.json) · [Silverstone slick change](https://www.formula1.com/en/video/2024-british-grand-prix-hamilton-re-takes-the-lead-after-pitting-ahead-of-norris.1803934075419823982)
- At Melbourne 2025, the offline model favored intermediates for Verstappen roughly **50 s before the reported stop** using a 20-second green benchmark. Estimating the earliest pit entry by subtracting the report's full lane duration leaves roughly **31 s** of margin. The delay and recommendation depend on which sectors have completed under usable flags. [Timing measurements](weather-crossover-timing.json) · [FIA driver debrief](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fia-post-race-press-conference-australia-2025.1638B6Cj4KD8xc21TCqQn8)
- Montreal 2024 checks the early full-wet advantage using both Haas cars against the intermediate runners. The Bahrain forecast check is a what-if with no cars on intermediates or wets. It exercises the first-mover branch using a user-supplied forecast and race priors that predate the replay. [F1 Montreal race report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-beats-norris-to-victory-in-thrilling-wet-dry-canadian-gp.6ytkbh7iDTWC3pJBwiaboI)

Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_weather_crossover.py -v` from `backend` for the offline fixtures. Rebuild the prior with `python -m scripts.build_crossover_history --cache-dir <path>`. Run `python scripts/check_weather_crossover.py` from the repository root for the API and browser workflow.
